## Milestone: first non-gamer playtest
Result:
- Controls required a few attempts but were learnable.
- Easy fish felt approachable.
- Rare fish created curiosity.
- Epic fish created challenge and satisfaction.
- Legendary fish felt aspirational.
- Upgrade loop was understood.
- Core minigame produced a visible positive reaction.
